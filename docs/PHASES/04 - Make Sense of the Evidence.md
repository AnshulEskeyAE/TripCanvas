## 5. THE CORE INSIGHT CHAIN

1. **Raw Observation:** Planners keep 15–25 browser tabs open and manually copy-paste details into custom Google Sheets with pros and cons.
2. **Why they do it:** No existing travel app (TripIt, Wanderlog, Google Flights) lets you test *speculative, unfinished options* across multiple websites side-by-side.
3. **The Hidden Cost Impact:** Headline prices hide checkout fees, making it impossible to see the true total cost of a choice until the final payment screen.
4. **The Consequence:** Planners either waste hours acting as "human middleware" across tabs, or they blow their budget, which directly ruins their fun and spending during the actual vacation.

## 8. DECISION
**GO (GATE 4 CLEARED)** — The evidence across all three sources points consistently to the same breakdown: **the speculative comparison gap** — no tool lets organizers test unfinished, multi-vendor option bundles side by side. This was clear enough to commit to Phase 05.

## 9. EXIT CRITERIA
Clear proof that the problem is real, painful, and already driving users to build makeshift spreadsheets and mental buffer margins.

## 10. FAILURE CONDITIONS
Picking a problem that only exists in user opinions rather than one backed by real coping behaviors (spreadsheets, buffer margins, on-trip cutbacks).

## 11. BACKTRACK CONDITIONS
If secondary research had shown that planners already love using existing tools like Wanderlog for early comparison (the research proved the exact opposite).
