## 4. WORK: THE OFFICIAL PROBLEM STATEMENT

> **For the lead trip organizer**  
> **in the pre-booking phase** (after dates and destination are decided),  
> **evaluating multi-item travel choices is an exhausting, trial-and-error chore**  
> **because** booking platforms operate in isolated vertical silos that hide true checkout costs and prevent side-by-side trade-off comparison between flights, stays, and arrival schedules,  
> **resulting in** hours spent manually managing complex spreadsheets, high cognitive fatigue, and unexpected checkout price creep that forces travelers into spending cutbacks during their vacation.

---

## 5. MANDATORY BOUNDARY DEFINITIONS

* **Who experiences this?**  
  Independent leisure travelers acting as the primary organizer/planner for solo trips, couples, or small groups.
* **Who does NOT experience this?**  
  Corporate/business travelers (travel portals book for them), all-inclusive resort or package tour buyers, and passive co-travelers who let others plan.
* **When does it occur?**  
  In the 2- to 14-day window between picking dates/destination and executing the final credit card payments.
* **What happens in reality?**  
  Organizers juggle 15–25 browser tabs across OTAs, airlines, and Google Maps. They repeatedly copy-paste links, prices, check-in windows, and pros/cons into spreadsheets to make sense of trade-offs.
* **Why does it happen (The Underlying Mechanism)?**  
  Booking sites (Google Flights, Airbnb, Booking.com) are engineered solely for closed single-transaction checkouts. They have zero cross-inventory awareness and hide drip fees (cleaning, resort fees, baggage) until the final screen.
* **How severe is it?**  
  - *Time:* 3 to 10 hours of repetitive manual cross-referencing per trip.  
  - *Financial:* $100 to $300 in unexpected checkout fees and secondary transit costs.  
  - *Emotional:* Severe planning fatigue and stress over making an irreversible booking mistake.
* **What is the consequence?**  
  Organizers suffer from analysis paralysis, and when booking costs exceed estimates, travelers are forced into "compensatory on-trip spending austerity"—sacrificing food, tours, and fun during the vacation.
* **What is their current workaround?**  
  Custom Google Sheets with pros/cons columns, Apple Notes link lists, and adding arbitrary mental "buffer margins" to prices.
